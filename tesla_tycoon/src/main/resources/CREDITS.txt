Seth Gotiya's petrol / diesel cars (v4), supplied by the author as "car for minecraft" (Blockbench glTF), "2021 Ford Mustang
Shelby GT500" (Blockbench OBJ) and "Lamborghini Aventador" (OBJ): converted the same way (re-oriented, scaled, wheels and glass
split, their own simple cabins replaced by the generated interiors). The originals are kept unchanged in assets/tesla_tycoon/models/.

Changes made (CC-BY-4.0 section 3(a)(1)(B)): the glTF meshes were converted to a game-ready format - node transforms baked,
re-oriented and scaled to blocks, wheels / glass / lights separated into parts, triangle counts reduced (Levels 1, 4 and 5),
the Level 5 emissive texture baked into vertex colours - and the wheel, steering, glass and light behaviour is added by the mod.
The delivery truck (back-alley scene) is generated from boxes and is not based on any third-party model.
The three banner / billboard / poster images are shipped unchanged as supplied by the author.

Voices (assets/tesla_tycoon/sounds/voice/*.ogg)
  Spoken with the Piper text-to-speech voices "hi_IN-pratham-medium" (Seth Gotiya) and "hi_IN-priyamvada-medium" (the customers),
  https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices - both licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/),
  trained on the AI4Bharat IndicNLP corpus by PravalX. Non-commercial use only. The lines are written for this mod
  (tools/gotiya_lines.py); Gotiya's clips are pitched down a little.

Voice chat (push-to-talk speech recognition)
  whisper-jni 1.7.1 (https://github.com/GiviMAD/whisper-jni), Apache License 2.0, bundled in the jar with its whisper.cpp natives
  (whisper.cpp by Georgi Gerganov, MIT licence). The Whisper model (OpenAI, MIT licence) is downloaded on first use from
  https://huggingface.co/ggerganov/whisper.cpp and verified by SHA-256; it is not part of the jar.
